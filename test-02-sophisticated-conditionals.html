Test 2: Sophisticated Conditionals

Part 1 covers Lessons 7-10; Part 2 reviews Lessons 1-10 cumulatively.

1 Part 1 — Sophisticated Conditionals

Review inversion in conditionals, conditional alternatives to if, hypothetical meaning without if, and advanced wish / if only.

1. ___ we consulted legal sooner, the whole dispute could have been avoided.

2. ___ the board reject the proposal, we would need to present an alternative by Friday.

3. (If I were you), I'd escalate this straight to the director.

4. (If you should need) any further documents, contact HR directly.

5. We won't extend the contract ___ the delivery times improve significantly.

6. The vendor accepted our terms, ___ we paid a deposit upfront.

7. You can leave early today, (as long as) you finish the handover notes first.

8. (Suppose) the funding falls through at the last minute — what's our backup plan?

9. Submit the form today; ___ you'll lose your place on the shortlist.

10. ___ her colleague's timely warning, she would have sent the invoice to the wrong client.

11. He approved the budget (without/double-check) the figures first.

12. Back up the files now; (otherwise) we risk losing everything overnight.

13. I wish I ___ the contract more carefully before signing it.

14. I wish he ___ interrupting every single client call.

15. If only we (escalate) it sooner, we could have kept the client.

16. I wish I'd flagged the risk, because if I had, we (not/lose) the account.

2 Part 2 — Cumulative Review: Sections 1 & 2

3. We (go) ahead with the launch in March, but the funding fell through.

4. The new packaging ___ reduce shipping costs by roughly ten percent.

5. She ___ cancelled the meeting — everyone had already read the memo.

6. He's out of breath — he (must/run) to catch the train.

7. ___ the board approved the merger sooner, the deal wouldn't have collapsed.

8. We'll approve the plan ___ the budget stays under control.

9. (But for) the backup generator, the entire server room would have gone dark.

10. I wish she ___ complaining about every small change to the schedule.

11. By the time we land, I (wait) at the gate for over an hour.

12. He ___ terrified of public speaking as a teenager. (a past state)

13. ___ the deadline move again, please notify every department immediately.

14. She signed the lease (without/read) the small print at all.

15. If only he ___ us about the delay earlier, we could have rearranged the schedule.

16. They (not need/book) early — plenty of seats were still free.
